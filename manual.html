How to play these silly games I made:

Snake

Guide the snake to collect hearts and spell our names!

Arrow Keys → Move
Eat hearts → Grow
Reach full length → You win my ♥️

Where is Your Man?

Navigate the maze and find me!

Arrow Keys → Move
Find me → Win
Minimap shows where our locations

Flappy Hearts

Float through the sky and collect hearts.

Space Bar → Flap
Collect hearts for points

Explorer

A work in progress

A / D → Move
W / Space → Jump
Left Click → Break blocks
Right Click → Place blocks
C → Crafting menu
1–5 → Select hotbar slot
